# Slab

A partition: a flat 26 × 5 × 14 box, grey until it has a key, then filled with that key's color and labeled on top (k1–k4). Slabs stack 5px apart.

**You provide** `stack` (bottom first: `k1`–`k4`, `idle`, `fact`), `scale` (default 2) and `labels`.

- Keys keep their order and colors: k1 blue, k2 green, k3 amber, k4 umber.
- Labels are white where white reads at 4.5:1 or better (k1, k2, k4) and ink on amber and fact slabs.
- Slabs travel on an arc that lifts 70px at its middle.
