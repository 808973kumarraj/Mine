# LED

A status light: a dot with a soft glow 4.5 times its radius and a small white highlight up and to the left.

**You provide** `color` (`green`, `amber` or `red`), `size` (radius: 1.7 on machines, 1.5 on rack tiles) and `animate`, which pulses the glow: green and amber slowly, red twice as fast.

- LEDs decorate machines and pills; the state they show is always said in words somewhere on screen.
