# ClosingCard

The end card: the series and episode, the lesson as a short serif title, an italic line under it, a dashed rule, the takeaways as pills and one line of config.

**You provide** `series`, `episode`, `title` (three or four words, ending with a period), `subtitle`, `takeaways` (`{ label, tone }`, two to four, in capitals) and `footnote`.

- The title is the whole episode in one imperative: "Move less data."
- In an episode it fades in over the last scene and the title prints with `StippleText` (serif, 1.4 s).
