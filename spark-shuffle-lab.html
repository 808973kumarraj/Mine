SETTINGS AND INPUTS

This is a live model of Spark Structured Streaming: each trigger starts a micro-batch, which runs a map stage, a shuffle and a reduce stage. One tile is a block of rows. Dark tiles are serialized bytes. Numbers come from a simple cost model and are illustrative, not benchmarks. Setting names and defaults are for Spark 3.x: spark.sql.shuffle.partitions = 200, adaptive target size 64 MB, skew threshold 256 MB and 5× the median. Each executor has 4 cores. A change to partitions, strategy or AQE applies from the next batch; everything else acts at once. Keys: Space pause, 1 to 7 scenarios, A S D F G H J K floods, W E T faults.
